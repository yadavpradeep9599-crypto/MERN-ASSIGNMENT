Why I Chose These Colours

I chose blue as the main colour because it looks clean and professional, and it is often used for technology websites.

The light background (#f4f6fb) and dark text (#222222) have strong contrast, so the text is easy to read.

I kept all colours in :root variables, so I can change the whole theme by editing one place.
